Research Focus

  • Atmospheric boundary-layer turbulence and scalar transport.
  • Aerospace applications involving high-Reynolds-number and transitional flows.
  • Biomimetic approaches for flow and boundary-layer problems.

Research Experience

Undergraduate Researcher, Atmospheric Boundary Layer Laboratory

March 2024 – Present.

  • Analysed turbulent-flow characteristics in atmospheric boundary layers, including Reynolds-stress tensor components and energy dissipation mechanisms.
  • Investigated flux-variance relationships and scalar-transport dissimilarity under varying atmospheric stability conditions.
  • Implemented quality-control and data-processing workflows for 10 Hz wind-observation tower data using Python and MATLAB.

Selected Projects

  • Structural and operational analysis of American Airlines Flight 587: examined vertical stabilizer failure, excessive rudder deflection, wake turbulence response, and training-program factors.
  • Aerodynamic and structural analysis of A-10 Thunderbolt II: analysed airfoil behaviour, structural materials, redundancy, survivability, and engine performance under damaged conditions.
  • Wind power and meteorological factors: built Python-based station-matching and time-series analysis workflows for wind-energy regions in South Korea.
  • Atmospheric optical-property modelling: modified Fortran models for optical depth, transmittance, spectral-line broadening, and water-vapor effects.
  • Atmospheric chemistry and pollution analysis: modelled ozone photochemistry and pollutant concentration changes with Fortran and Python.
