- **Structural and operational analysis of American Airlines Flight 587:** examined vertical stabilizer failure, excessive rudder deflection, wake turbulence response, and training-program factors.

- **Wind power and meteorological factors:** built Python-based station-matching and time-series analysis workflows for wind-energy regions in South Korea.
- **Atmospheric optical-property modelling:** modified Fortran models for optical depth, transmittance, spectral-line broadening, and water-vapor effects.
- **Atmospheric chemistry and pollution analysis:** modelled ozone photochemistry and pollutant concentration changes with Fortran and Python.
