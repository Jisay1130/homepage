I am a master's student in **Aerospace Engineering**, with a bachelor's degree in **Atmospheric Science**. My research connects atmospheric processes with computational fluid dynamics to study wind and turbulence around urban buildings.

I work with **WRF, PALM, and OpenFOAM**, alongside Python and geospatial modelling tools. My research interests include weather-informed multiscale modelling, building-resolving large-eddy simulation, and surrogate models for efficient wind prediction.

## Research interests

- **Weather-informed urban flow:** connecting meteorological conditions to building-scale wind and turbulence.
- **Urban wind and low-altitude aviation:** investigating wind environments around buildings and UAM vertiports.
- **Surrogate and reduced-order modelling:** exploring data-driven approaches to accelerate flow prediction.

## Background

My atmospheric-science background informs how I interpret boundary-layer processes and meteorological conditions. My graduate training in aerospace engineering extends this perspective to fluid mechanics, numerical modelling, and engineering applications.
