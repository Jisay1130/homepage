# Computational Fluid Dynamics · Turbulence · Atmospheric Boundary Layer

전산유체역학, 난류 모델링, 대기경계층 관측자료 분석을 바탕으로 항공우주 및 환경 유동 문제를 연구합니다.

I work on **computational fluid dynamics**, **turbulent flow modelling**, and **atmospheric boundary-layer analysis** for aerospace and environmental flow problems. My background connects atmospheric science, numerical modelling, and aerospace applications.

My current work focuses on turbulence structure, flux-variance relationships, and high-frequency wind-observation data processing. I use **Python**, **Fortran**, **MATLAB**, and Linux-based workflows for analysis and modelling.

## Current Research Topics

- **Turbulent flow modelling:** Reynolds-stress structure, scalar transport, and boundary-layer behaviour.
- **Aerospace CFD:** high-Reynolds-number and transitional flows, with interest in atmospheric-entry and boundary-layer phenomena.
- **Atmospheric data processing:** quality control and analysis of high-frequency wind-observation tower data.

## Highlights

- Undergraduate Researcher, Atmospheric Boundary Layer Laboratory, March 2024 – Present.
- Project experience in aircraft accident analysis, aerodynamic/structural analysis, wind-energy data analysis, atmospheric radiation, and ozone photochemistry.

## Direction

I am interested in connecting field-observation data with CFD and reduced-order modelling so that complex flow behaviour can be interpreted, simulated, and eventually predicted more efficiently.
