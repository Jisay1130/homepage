My work is organised around one question: **how can observation data and numerical modelling be combined to understand turbulent flows that matter for aerospace and atmospheric applications?**

## Overview

My research direction sits between atmospheric observation, numerical modelling, and aerospace fluid mechanics. The near-term goal is to build reliable workflows for interpreting measured turbulence and connecting those measurements to simulation and engineering analysis.

## Research Topics

### Turbulence and Boundary-Layer Analysis

At the Atmospheric Boundary Layer Laboratory, I have worked on turbulence structure analysis using high-frequency wind-observation tower data. This includes Reynolds-stress tensor components, energy dissipation mechanisms, and scalar-transport behaviour under different atmospheric stability conditions.

**Tools:** Python · MATLAB · turbulence statistics · quality-control workflows

### Computational Fluid Dynamics

I am interested in high-Reynolds-number and transitional flows in aerospace applications, including boundary-layer phenomena and atmospheric-entry contexts. My project work has included aerodynamic and structural analysis of aircraft systems and aviation accident analysis.

**Tools:** CFD workflows · Fortran · Linux shell scripting · numerical modelling

### Atmospheric and Environmental Modelling

I have also worked with atmospheric and environmental datasets, including wind-power generation analysis, atmospheric optical-property modelling, and photochemical trend analysis using Fortran and Python.

**Tools:** Python · Fortran · GIS-style station matching · time-series analysis

## Selected Projects

- **American Airlines Flight 587 analysis:** vertical stabilizer failure, rudder deflection, wake turbulence response, and pilot workload considerations.
- **A-10 Thunderbolt II analysis:** NACA 6716 airfoil behaviour, structural-material choices, survivability, and engine performance under damaged conditions.
- **Wind-power and meteorological factors:** turbine and AWS station matching, seasonal wind-power trends, and meteorological drivers in South Korean wind-energy regions.
- **Atmospheric optical and chemical modelling:** radiative-transfer properties, spectral-line broadening, water-vapor effects, and ozone photochemistry.

## Research Keywords

CFD · turbulent flow modelling · atmospheric boundary layer · scalar transport · high-Reynolds-number flow · transition · radiative transfer · ozone photochemistry · Python · Fortran · MATLAB
