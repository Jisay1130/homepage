My research is organised around a central question: **how can meteorological information support reliable and computationally efficient predictions of urban wind and turbulence?**

## Weather-informed multiscale modelling

I study the connection between time-varying meteorological conditions and building-scale flow using WRF and PALM. My interests include boundary conditions, urban geometry, and the interpretation of wind and turbulence statistics.

**Methods and tools:** WRF · PALM LES · Python · geospatial preprocessing.

## Urban wind and engineering applications

I investigate flow around urban buildings, with an interest in pedestrian-level wind and wind environments relevant to UAM and vertiports. OpenFOAM and urban-geometry tools support my work on local flow modelling and CFD case preparation.

**Methods and tools:** OpenFOAM · QGIS · Blender · Linux · MPI.

## Surrogate and reduced-order prediction

I am interested in combining high-fidelity simulations with surrogate models to reduce prediction cost. This includes Gaussian process regression and proper orthogonal decomposition, with particular attention to testing predictions on conditions not used during model development.

**Methods and tools:** Python · Gaussian process regression · proper orthogonal decomposition.
