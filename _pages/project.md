## 2024

### Atmospheric Boundary Layer Laboratory

Undergraduate Researcher, March 2024 – Present.

- Analysed turbulence structure in atmospheric boundary-layer observations.
- Investigated flux-variance relationships under varying atmospheric stability conditions.
- Processed 10 Hz wind-observation tower data with Python and MATLAB workflows.

### Aerospace Engineering Coursework Projects

- **American Airlines Flight 587 analysis:** vertical stabilizer failure, excessive rudder deflection, wake turbulence response, and pilot workload considerations.
- **A-10 Thunderbolt II analysis:** airfoil behaviour, structural materials, survivability, redundancy, and damaged-condition engine performance.

### Atmospheric Science and Modelling Projects

- **Wind power and meteorological factors:** station matching, wind-power time-series analysis, and meteorological drivers in South Korean wind-energy regions.
- **Atmospheric optical-property modelling:** Fortran-based optical depth and transmittance modelling with Python visualisation.
- **Atmospheric chemistry and ozone photochemistry:** numerical modelling and visualisation of ozone and related species concentration changes.
